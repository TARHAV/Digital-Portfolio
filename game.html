Abstract

This is the first game I have made using bitsy. I chose to make a game about how humanity's idea of gravity changed through time. It was a challenge to use. The website is designed to be very limited in what you can do as a game designer, however there is a reason for this. The creator of Bitsy wanted a simple game engine he could use on his phone during his commute to work in the morning. I struggled to make a densely packed game both visually and contextually. The story and graphics I made for the game were small but efficient. Eventually, I was able to utilize the small 16x16 grid I had to make a fun and short game. I was also able to utilize the simple 3-bit color limitation to convey different objects and characters.
